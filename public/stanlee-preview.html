Our Story

Started in a garage. Built for the world.

"We were tired of bottles that leaked, warmed up, and wore out. So we built one that wouldn't."

Stanlee was founded in 2019 by two engineers who couldn't find a bottle worth keeping. Three years and 47 prototypes later, we shipped 500 bottles. They sold out in 6 hours.

Today, Stanlee is carried by doctors, athletes, chefs, and parents across 42 countries. The obsession with getting it right hasn't changed.
